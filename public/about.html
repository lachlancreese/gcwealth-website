Our story

GC Wealth began in 2024 with a simple idea: most people don't need a loan processed, they need a plan. The right loan depends on what you're buying, what comes after it, and how your income and family will change over time.

From that starting point we've grown into a specialist team covering residential lending, investment finance, construction, commercial property, SMSF lending and asset finance. Clients come to us for a first home, a portfolio, a business premises or a piece of equipment, and many come back for the next step.

We're not here to process loans. We're here to build financial futures, and to be honest with you about what's realistic along the way.

What makes us different

  • More lenders, better options

    We compare 70+ lenders on our panel, from the major banks to specialist lenders, rather than one bank's products.

  • Qualified and accountable

    Every GC Wealth broker is accredited with the MFAA, and we're required by law to act in your best interests.

  • Boutique service, national reach

    We're based in Southport, with an office in Hampton, VIC, and help clients Australia-wide.

  • We work for you

    We'll tell you if waiting, saving a little more or staying with your current lender is the better move.
