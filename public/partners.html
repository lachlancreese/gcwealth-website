How a referral works

The same steps every time, so you always know where your client is up to.

  1. Agreement in place

    We set up a written referral agreement with your business first. It records how we work together and any referral arrangements, which are disclosed to your client as the law requires.

  2. You refer

    Use the form below or call us, once your client has agreed to you sharing their details.

  3. We make contact

    A broker contacts your client and lets you know the first conversation has happened.

  4. Milestone updates

    With your client's consent, we update you when the application is lodged, when it's approved, and at settlement.

Who we work with

Referral relationships work well when both sides understand each other's world. We regularly work with:

  • Accountants and financial plannersBusiness owners, self-employed clients, investors and SMSFs. For SMSF lending we take our instructions from the client's planner, accountant or solicitor.
  • Buyer's agents and real estate agentsPre-approvals before clients make offers, and finance that keeps contracts on track to settlement.
  • Builders and developersConstruction and house and land finance, with progress payments set up around your contract.
  • Solicitors and conveyancersClear communication through to settlement, including bridging and complex structures.
