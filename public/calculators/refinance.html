How this calculator works

We work out the monthly principal and interest repayment at each rate over your remaining term, then divide your switching costs by the monthly difference to see how long they'd take to recover.

Assumptions

  • Both rates stay the same for the rest of the term, and the loan term doesn't change.
  • Ongoing fees, cashback offers and features such as offset accounts aren't included, though they can matter as much as the rate.
