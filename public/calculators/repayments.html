How this calculator works

Principal and interest repayments are worked out so the loan is fully repaid over the term, with interest charged on the balance each period. Interest-only repayments cover the interest alone, so the balance doesn't fall until the interest-only period ends and principal and interest repayments start. Moneysmart explains how interest-only loans work.

Fortnightly and weekly figures spread the same loan over 26 or 52 repayments a year. Some lenders instead set fortnightly repayments at half the monthly amount, which repays the loan a little faster.

Assumptions

  • The interest rate stays the same for the whole term. In practice variable rates move, and fixed rates end.
  • Fees and charges aren't included.
  • Results are rounded to the nearest dollar.

Questions about repayments

Why does the calculator ask me for a rate?

Rates differ widely between lenders and borrowers, so the most accurate figure is the one from your own loan offer or statement. If you don't have one, a broker can tell you what rates are available for your situation.

How does the RBA cash rate affect my repayments?

Lenders set their own rates, but most variable rates move when the cash rate changes. Use the rate rise rows above to see what an increase would mean for you. See the current cash rate.
