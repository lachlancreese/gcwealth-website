Steven Shaw

Finance Specialist

  • Self-employed borrowers
  • Property investors
  • Time-poor professionals

Steven spent 12 years working across some of Australia's major banks, with experience spanning home lending, business banking and private banking. For the past three years he has worked on the broking side of the industry, including two years leading the lending team at a strategic property portfolio company.

That combination gives him a view of lending from both sides: how banks assess clients, and how finance fits into the bigger picture of building and managing wealth through property.

Steven particularly enjoys working with people whose finances don't fit neatly into one box. He specialises in helping self-employed clients who need more than a home or business loan and want someone who can understand and coordinate their broader lending. He also works closely with professional property investors on their lending and how it fits their portfolio, and with time-poor professionals who value a private-bank level of service without being tied to one bank.

His focus is on building genuine, long-term relationships and being the person clients can turn to when they need someone who understands the bigger picture.

Outside work, Steven is married with a young son, is a bit of a vehicle enthusiast, and enjoys getting away camping with the family.
