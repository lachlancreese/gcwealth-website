Government help for first home buyers

Checked against official sources in November 2026.

Help comes from two places. The Australian Government's 5% Deposit Scheme works the same way across the country. Your state or territory may add a grant for a new home and a cut in stamp duty, and those rules differ a lot depending on where you buy.

Australian Government 5% Deposit Scheme

Under this scheme the government guarantees part of your loan, so eligible first home buyers can buy with a deposit from 5% without paying lenders mortgage insurance. Since 1 October 2025 there are no income limits and no cap on places. Property price caps apply, and the Gold Coast is one of the regional centres with a higher cap. You apply through a participating lender, which we can arrange.

Grants and stamp duty, state by state

Where are you buying?

New South Wales

First home owner grant
$10,000 on a new home

The First Home Owner (New Homes) Grant pays $10,000 towards a new, off-the-plan or substantially renovated home priced up to $600,000, or a land and building contract up to $750,000 combined. There's no grant for established homes.

Stamp duty
No duty up to $800,000; reduced up to $1 million

Under the First Home Buyers Assistance Scheme, eligible first home buyers pay no transfer duty on a new or existing home valued up to $800,000, and a reduced amount up to $1,000,000. For vacant land to build a first home, there's no duty up to $350,000 and a reduced amount up to $450,000. You need to move in within 12 months and live there for at least 12 months.

Work out NSW stamp duty Official sources: Revenue NSW (grant), Revenue NSW (duty).

Victoria

First home owner grant
$10,000 on a new home

The First Home Owner Grant pays $10,000 towards buying or building a new home valued up to $750,000. There's no grant for established homes.

Stamp duty
No duty up to $600,000; reduced up to $750,000

Eligible first home buyers pay no duty on a home valued up to $600,000, and a reduced amount up to $750,000. Separately, if you'll live in the home, the principal place of residence concession lowers the rates on homes valued up to $550,000.

Work out VIC stamp duty Official sources: State Revenue Office Victoria (grant), State Revenue Office Victoria (duty).

Queensland

First home owner grant
$30,000 on a new home

The First Home Owner Grant pays $30,000 towards buying or building a new home valued under $750,000, including land. The Queensland Government has confirmed it continues for eligible contracts signed from 1 July 2026. There's no grant for established homes.

Stamp duty
No duty on a new home; established homes up to $700,000

First home buyers of an established home can claim a concession that removes duty on homes valued up to $700,000, reducing in steps until it ends at $800,000. Since 1 May 2025, first home buyers of a new home, or vacant land to build one, can claim a full concession with no price cap. From 1 August 2026, home concessions are generally only available to Australian citizens, permanent residents and specified foreign retirees.

Work out QLD stamp duty Official sources: Queensland Government (grant), Queensland Revenue Office (duty).

Western Australia

First home owner grant
$10,000 on a new home

The First Home Owner Grant pays $10,000 towards buying or building a new home. For transactions from 7 May 2026, the home can be valued up to $800,000 south of the 26th parallel, which includes Perth, or $1,000,000 further north. There's no grant for established homes.

Stamp duty
No duty up to $600,000; reduced up to $800,000

For transactions from 7 May 2026, eligible first home buyers pay no duty on a home valued up to $600,000, and a concessional rate ($16.15 per $100 above $600,000) up to $800,000. For vacant land to build a first home, there's no duty up to $450,000, with a concession up to $550,000.

Work out WA stamp duty Official sources: Government of Western Australia (grant), RevenueWA (duty).

South Australia

First home owner grant
Up to $15,000 on a new home

The First Home Owner Grant pays up to $15,000 towards buying or building a new home, including an off-the-plan apartment or a substantially renovated home, with no cap on its value. There's no grant for established homes.

Stamp duty
No duty on a new home; none on established homes

For contracts from 6 June 2024, eligible first home buyers pay no stamp duty on a new home, an off-the-plan apartment or vacant land to build a home, with no price cap. There's no first home buyer concession on an established home.

Work out SA stamp duty Official sources: RevenueSA (grant), RevenueSA (duty).

Tasmania

First home owner grant
$20,000 on a new home

For transactions that start between 1 July 2026 and 30 June 2027, the First Home Owner Grant is $20,000 towards building or buying a new home, with no cap on its value. It's set to return to $10,000 after that.

Stamp duty
No concession (the established-home exemption ended 30 June 2026)

Tasmania's duty exemption for first home buyers of established homes ended for transfers settling after 30 June 2026, so first home buyers now pay the standard rates.

Work out TAS stamp duty Official sources: Tasmanian legislation (grant), State Revenue Office Tasmania (duty).

Australian Capital Territory

First home owner grant
No grant

The ACT doesn't offer a first home owner grant. Help comes through the Home Buyer Concession Scheme on duty instead.

Stamp duty
No duty if you haven't owned property in five years

From 1 July 2026, the Home Buyer Concession Scheme removes duty for eligible buyers, with no income test or price cap. You and your partner can't have owned property in the last five years, and you need to live in the home. It covers new homes, established homes and vacant residential land. Owner-occupiers who aren't eligible pay the lower owner-occupier rates.

Work out ACT stamp duty Official sources: ACT Revenue Office (grant), ACT Revenue Office (duty).

Northern Territory

First home owner grant
$50,000 on a new home

The HomeGrown Territory Grant pays $50,000 to first home buyers who build or buy a new home, including off the plan, with no cap on the price. Contracts need to be signed between 1 October 2024 and 30 September 2027. The $10,000 grant for established homes has closed.

Stamp duty
No duty concession; grants and a house and land exemption

The Northern Territory has no general first home buyer duty concession. A stamp duty exemption applies to eligible house and land packages for contracts signed between 1 July 2022 and 30 June 2027, and first home buyers may be eligible for grants. Check the Territory Revenue Office for current eligibility.

Work out NT stamp duty Official sources: Northern Territory Government (grant), Territory Revenue Office (duty).

Grants, concessions and price caps change, and each has its own rules on residency, moving in and how long you live there. We check your eligibility against the current rules when we talk. The official source for each state is linked above, and you can check the 5% Deposit Scheme with Housing Australia.

How much deposit do you need?

Many lenders prefer a 20% deposit, because below that you'll usually pay lenders mortgage insurance (LMI). A smaller deposit is still possible with LMI, through the 5% Deposit Scheme, or with a family guarantee.

Budget for upfront costs too: transfer duty if a concession doesn't cover it, conveyancing or legal fees, building and pest inspections, and lender fees.

Questions first home buyers ask

Can I buy my first home with less than a 20% deposit?

Often, yes. Options include paying lenders mortgage insurance, the Australian Government 5% Deposit Scheme if you're eligible, or a family guarantee. Each has different costs and conditions, and we'll compare them with you.

Does the First Home Owner Grant apply to established homes?

No. Every state and territory that offers a grant limits it to new homes, including homes bought off the plan. You may still be able to get a stamp duty concession on an established home, depending on where you buy.

Which state has the biggest first home grant?

The Northern Territory's HomeGrown Territory Grant pays $50,000 for a new home. Queensland is next at $30,000, then Tasmania at $20,000 and South Australia at up to $15,000. New South Wales, Victoria and Western Australia pay $10,000, and the ACT has no grant.

Can I use the grant and the 5% Deposit Scheme together?

Often, yes, if you meet the criteria for each. They're separate programs with their own rules, so we check both before you sign a contract.

How long does pre-approval last?

Usually around 90 days, depending on the lender, and it can often be renewed. Read about pre-approval.
