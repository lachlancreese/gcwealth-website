Home loan pre-approval

Pre-approval tells you how much a lender is likely to lend before you make an offer, so you can negotiate or bid at auction with confidence.

At a glance

  • A conditional approval based on your finances
  • Usually lasts around 90 days, depending on the lender
  • Can often be renewed
  • Not a final approval or a guarantee

Last updated 17 November 2026

What pre-approval is, and isn't

Pre-approval (also called conditional approval) means a lender has looked at your income, expenses, debts and credit history and is prepared to lend up to a certain amount, subject to conditions. It gives you a firm budget and shows sellers and agents you're serious.

It isn't a final approval. Before the loan is formally approved the lender will still need:

  • A satisfactory valuation of the property you choose
  • The signed contract of sale
  • Confirmation that your circumstances haven't changed
  • Any other conditions listed in the pre-approval

Buying at auction

Auction sales are unconditional, so you can't make the purchase subject to finance. Get pre-approval first, and talk to us before you bid about the property, your limit, and any conditions that still need to be met.

How we help

  • Work out a realistic budget, including purchase costs
  • Choose the lender most likely to suit you, so you don't need multiple applications
  • Keep your pre-approval current while you look
  • Move quickly to formal approval once you find a property

What you'll need

  • Photo ID
  • Recent payslips, or tax returns if you're self-employed
  • Bank statements showing savings and regular expenses
  • Details of any debts

Questions about pre-approval

Is pre-approval a guarantee I'll get the loan?

No. The lender can still decline or change the offer if the property doesn't value up, the contract has unusual terms, or your circumstances change. Pre-approval reduces uncertainty; it doesn't remove it.

How long does pre-approval last?

Usually around 90 days, depending on the lender. It can often be renewed with updated documents.

Does pre-approval affect my credit score?

Many lenders run a credit check at pre-approval, which is recorded on your credit file. That's why we choose the lender carefully up front rather than applying with several.
