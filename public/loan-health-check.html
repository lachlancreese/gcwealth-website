Complimentary loan health check

Find out whether your home loan is still the right fit. We compare it with options from our panel of 70+ lenders, count the costs of any change, and tell you honestly whether switching, restructuring or staying put makes sense.

Start my health check Call GC Wealth

What it takes

  • Six quick questions about your current loan
  • No documents needed to start
  • A conversation with a broker about your options
  • Complimentary, with no obligation to change anything

Last updated 17 November 2026

What we check

  • Rate and fees compared with current options across our panel, including your existing lender
  • Features such as offset, redraw and the ability to make extra repayments
  • Structure: fixed or variable, interest-only or principal and interest, and whether home and investment debt are separated
  • The costs of changing: discharge fees, break costs on fixed loans, and new lender fees
  • Your plans: renovating, investing, a fixed rate ending, or simply keeping costs down

Questions about the health check

Will this affect my credit score?

No. A health check is a conversation and a comparison. Nothing is lodged with a lender unless you decide to go ahead.

What if staying with my lender is the better option?

Then we'll tell you. Sometimes the right move is asking your current lender for a better rate, or simply changing the loan's structure.

What should I have ready?

Nothing to start. When we talk, a recent loan statement helps, along with a rough idea of your income and any other debts.
