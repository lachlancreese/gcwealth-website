How a referral works

The same steps every time, so you always know where your client is up to.

  1. Agreement in place

    We set up a written referral agreement with your business first. It records how we work together and any referral arrangements, which are disclosed to your client as the law requires.

  2. You refer

    Use the form below or call us, once your client has agreed to you sharing their details.

  3. We make contact

    A broker contacts your client and lets you know the first conversation has happened.

  4. Milestone updates

    With your client's consent, we update you when the application is lodged, when it's approved, and at settlement.
