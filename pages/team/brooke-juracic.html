As co-founder and Managing Director of GC Wealth, Brooke Juracic brings a strategic, education-driven approach to finance broking that prioritises structure, clarity and long-term wealth creation. Overseeing a diverse portfolio ranging from everyday personal lending to large-scale developments, Brooke and her team are dedicated to taking the complexity out of borrowing. Her industry background spans more than a decade across financial advisory administration, executive brokerage operations, residential construction, and owning and operating mortgage broking franchises. That gives her a 360-degree view of how lenders assess risk, how properties are built and valued, and how complex loans are structured from pre-approval through to settlement.

A core pillar of Brooke's practice is self-managed super fund (SMSF) lending. She works closely with existing SMSF property investors to review and restructure their current loans, helping keep debt reduction on track so properties can be paid off before retirement. With the recent changes restricting new SMSF borrowing to business real property, Brooke also helps business owners and investors buy commercial property within their super funds with confidence and compliance.

Above all, Brooke is driven by a genuine passion for financial literacy. She believes understanding how money and lending work shouldn't be reserved for seasoned investors, and makes it her mission to explain the mechanics of every client's financial structure so they can build lasting wealth. From seasoned portfolio builders to young people taking their first steps into finance, Brooke is committed to giving people the practical knowledge and habits to set themselves up for lifelong financial success.
