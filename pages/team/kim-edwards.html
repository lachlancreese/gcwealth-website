With a finance career dating back to 2011, Kim Edwards brings extensive industry insight and deep institutional knowledge to her clients. Having held senior roles as a Lending Specialist and Home Finance Manager at two major Australian banks, alongside broad experience with several other major banks, Kim understands how lenders assess applications from the inside out. She uses that background to help property investors refinance, unlock equity and structure their lending for sustainable portfolio growth.

Kim also works through complex financial structures for self-employed borrowers, business owners and corporate entities. With deep expertise in alt doc and low doc lending, she takes the friction out of borrowing for clients whose finances sit outside standard lending criteria, making sure their business revenue and broader financial picture are accurately represented to lenders.

Beyond residential and investment lending, Kim has a specialised niche in low doc lending for property developers. This includes structuring funding for land acquisitions before a development application (DA) is lodged, giving developers the agility to secure sites and move projects forward with confidence.
