What commercial lenders look at

  • The property: its type, location, condition and how easily it could be re-let or sold.
  • The lease: how long is left, the tenant's strength, and the rent compared to the market.
  • Income cover: whether the rent, or your business's profit if you occupy it, comfortably covers the repayments.
  • You and your business: financial statements, trading history and experience.

Lenders usually lend a smaller share of a commercial property's value than they would for a home, so you'll typically need a larger deposit or extra security. Loan terms are often shorter, and some loans are reviewed every few years.

Questions about commercial property loans

How much deposit do I need for commercial property?

Usually more than for a home, because lenders lend a lower share of the value. The exact amount depends on the property, the lease and your finances. Equity in other property can sometimes be used as extra security.

Can I use my home as security for a commercial loan?

Often, yes. It can reduce the cash deposit you need, but it also puts your home at risk if the loan isn't repaid, so it's worth weighing carefully.

Does GST apply when buying commercial property?

It can. Whether GST applies, and whether you can claim it back, depends on the sale and your registration. Check with your accountant before you sign.
