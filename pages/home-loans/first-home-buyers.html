Government help for Queensland first home buyers

Checked against official sources in November 2026.

First Home Owner Grant: $30,000 for new homes

Eligible first home buyers can receive $30,000 towards buying or building a new home in Queensland valued at less than $750,000, including land. The Queensland Government has confirmed the $30,000 grant continues for eligible contracts signed from 1 July 2026. There's no grant for established homes.

Transfer (stamp) duty concessions

Since 1 May 2025, eligible first home buyers who buy a new home, or vacant land to build their first home on, can claim a full concession that reduces transfer duty to nil. A separate first home concession can reduce or remove duty on established homes, depending on the price.

To claim a home concession, buyers need to be Australian citizens, permanent residents or specified foreign retirees, and there are rules about moving in and living in the home.

Australian Government 5% Deposit Scheme

Under this scheme the government guarantees part of your loan, so eligible first home buyers can buy with a deposit from 5% without paying lenders mortgage insurance. Since 1 October 2025 there are no income limits and no cap on places. Property price caps apply, and the Gold Coast is one of the regional centres with a higher cap. You apply through a participating lender, which we can arrange.

Grants, concessions and price caps change. We check your eligibility against the current rules when we talk. You can also confirm with the Queensland Government, the Queensland Revenue Office and Housing Australia.

How much deposit do you need?

Many lenders prefer a 20% deposit, because below that you'll usually pay lenders mortgage insurance (LMI). A smaller deposit is still possible with LMI, through the 5% Deposit Scheme, or with a family guarantee.

Budget for upfront costs too: transfer duty if a concession doesn't cover it, conveyancing or legal fees, building and pest inspections, and lender fees.

Questions first home buyers ask

Can I buy my first home with less than a 20% deposit?

Often, yes. Options include paying lenders mortgage insurance, the Australian Government 5% Deposit Scheme if you're eligible, or a family guarantee. Each has different costs and conditions, and we'll compare them with you.

Does the First Home Owner Grant apply to established homes?

No. In Queensland the grant is for new homes only. You may still be able to claim a transfer duty concession on an established home.

Can I use the grant and the 5% Deposit Scheme together?

Often, yes, if you meet the criteria for each. They're separate programs with their own rules, so we check both before you sign a contract.

How long does pre-approval last?

Usually around 90 days, depending on the lender, and it can often be renewed. Read about pre-approval.
