What the cash rate means for your loan

The cash rate is what banks pay to borrow from each other overnight. It isn't the rate on your home loan, but lenders usually move their variable rates when it changes. Fixed rates are priced on where markets expect rates to go, so they can move before or after a decision.

Questions about the cash rate

How often does the RBA meet?

The Reserve Bank's Monetary Policy Board meets eight times a year and announces each decision at 2.30pm (Sydney time) on the second day of the meeting.

Will my lender pass on a change?

Each lender decides for itself, and the timing varies. Your lender will tell you about any change to your rate and repayments.

Should I fix my rate?

It depends on your plans and how much certainty you want. Fixing gives predictable repayments but usually limits extra repayments and can mean break costs if you leave early. A broker can compare fixed, variable and split options for you.
