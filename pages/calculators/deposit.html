How much deposit do you need?

Many lenders prefer 20%, because below that you'll usually pay lenders mortgage insurance. A smaller deposit is still possible: with mortgage insurance, through the Australian Government 5% Deposit Scheme for eligible first home buyers, or with a family guarantee.

Lenders also look for genuine savings, usually money you've built up over at least a few months, so a steady saving pattern helps your application as well as your deposit.

Assumptions

  • You save the same amount every month. Interest on your savings isn't included.
  • Property prices don't change while you save.
