How this calculator works

We work out your current monthly repayment, then follow the loan month by month: interest is charged on the balance (less any offset balance), and your repayment plus any extra comes off. The lump sum is paid at the start.

Assumptions

  • The rate and the offset balance stay the same for the life of the loan.
  • Your required repayment doesn't change when you pay extra. Some lenders recalculate it.
  • Fees, and any limits on extra repayments (common on fixed rates), aren't included.

Questions

Offset or redraw: what's the difference?

Both reduce interest. An offset is a separate account linked to the loan, while redraw lets you take back extra repayments you've made. They can be treated differently for tax on investment loans, so check with your accountant.

Can I make extra repayments on a fixed rate?

Often only up to a limit each year, and some fixed loans don't allow them at all. Splitting a loan into fixed and variable parts is a common way to keep flexibility.
